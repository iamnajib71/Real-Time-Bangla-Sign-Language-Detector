# Response to ScienceOpen editorial screening

Dear ScienceOpen Editorial Team,

Thank you for reviewing the submission and identifying these points. The manuscript has been revised as follows.

**1. Missing ROR identifier for the affiliation**

The affiliation on the title page now reads Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North South University, Dhaka, Bangladesh, with ROR **https://ror.org/05wdbfp45**. The identifier was checked against the official ROR record, which lists North South University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. This same identifier is supplied for the affiliation ROR field during resubmission; it is distinct from the author's ORCID.

**2. Insufficient breadth and currency of the literature review**

Section 2 has been expanded to discuss three connected areas: recognition versus continuous signing and translation; Bangla datasets and recent temporal models; and representation choices and accessibility evaluation. The bibliography has increased from 4 to 14 references, with one self-reference. New sources include neural translation and joint recognition/translation, a continuous-recognition review, BAUST Lipi, BdSLW401, recent Bangla word- and bounded sentence-recognition studies, MediaPipe Hands, and interdisciplinary guidance on sign-language technology. The preprocessing method now also cites Otsu's original work.

Each new DOI was verified against Crossref or DataCite metadata, with the content checked through publisher or author sources. The review distinguishes static alphabets, isolated words, whole-clip sentence classification and continuous translation rather than comparing their accuracy figures directly. arXiv sources are explicitly identified as preprints. The manuscript explains how this study differs from those approaches and retains its limitations on signer independence and conversational translation.

The experimental measurements, data partitions, model weights and predictions are unchanged. A clean manuscript and a marked comparison against the previously prepared submission are provided. The unchanged original ScienceOpen preprint remains preserved separately.

Thank you for considering the revised submission.

Nazmul Hassan
